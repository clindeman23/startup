Step 1: Brainstorm and Research

Find a problem worth solving, then learn what already exists.

1A

Step 1.1: Brainstorm Ideas

Think of problems that people face in daily life that could be solved with some innovation.

Ask yourself

  • What problems do people face every day?
  • What products or services do I wish existed?
  • What frustrates me that could be fixed with a simple idea?
  • What’s something people already use that could be improved?
  • What hobbies, interests, or trends inspire me right now?

Guidelines

  • You can create any kind of business.
  • You cannot copy an existing business or product exactly.
  • If your idea is similar to something that exists, you must explain what makes yours unique or better.

Step 2: Design an Initial Mock-Up

2A

Step 2.1: Write a Product Proposal

0
AI Free

You and your team will be creating a 1-page typed “proposal” for your company. It will include 5 paragraphs. Each paragraph is going to define a different part of your company.

  1. Business Overview (The What)

    • What is your product, service, or invention?
    • How does it work or what does it do?
    • Who is your target audience (who would buy or use it)?

  2. Purpose and Problem (The Why)

    • What problem does your idea solve, or what need does it fill?
    • Why is this problem worth solving?
    • How will your idea make life easier, better, or more fun for people?

  3. Originality (The Difference)

    • What makes your idea unique or better than similar ideas that already exist?
    • How does your idea stand out from the competition?
    • If something similar exists, what’s your twist or improvement?

  4. Team Vision (The Who)

    • Who is in your group and what are each person’s main roles?
    • How will your team work together to make this project successful?

  5. Next Steps (The Plan)

    • What’s the next thing your team will work on to bring this idea to life (for example: designing a prototype, creating visuals, making your brand board)?

Formatting tips

  • Must be at least one full page (or around 4 to 5 well-developed paragraphs).
  • Use clear, professional language. You can imagine you are pitching your idea to real investors.
  • Each team member’s name should be listed at the top.
  • Be creative!

Step 2.2: Create a Sketch or Blueprint

2
AI Feedback

What is a Blueprint?

A blueprint is a detailed plan or drawing that shows your product’s design, parts, and purpose. It helps others understand how your idea would actually look and function in real life.

Your Blueprint Must Include

  1. Labeled Diagram
    • Draw or digitally design your product.
    • Label all important parts, features, or sections.
    • Use arrows, callouts, or notes to explain how things work.
  2. Function Notes: Under your drawing, write 3 to 5 short sentences explaining:
    • How your product functions or operates.
    • What materials or technology it might use.
    • How it helps your target user or solves a problem.
  3. Design Details: Include information such as:
    • Size or dimensions (approximate if needed)
    • Colors or materials
    • Moving parts, buttons, screens, sensors, etc.
  4. Optional Digital Add-Ons: If creating digitally, you may add:
    • Icons or mock buttons
    • Text boxes or arrows
    • Color highlights for key features

Step 3: Design a Company

For this step, in a Google doc, you will grow your company to have 4 main parts.

1
AI Planning

Name

  • Choose a name that matches your product and its purpose.
  • It should be short, creative, and easy to remember.
  • Avoid names that copy real companies.

Motto

  • Write a short phrase (5 to 8 words) that captures your company’s mission or attitude.
  • It should make people remember your brand and what it stands for.

Mission Statement

Write 3 to 5 sentences explaining why your company exists and what it believes in. You can include:

  • The problem you want to solve
  • The values your company stands for (innovation, sustainability, creativity, etc.)
  • Your company’s overall goal

Color Scheme

  • Pick 3 to 4 main colors that represent your brand.
  • Think about what feelings or ideas those colors communicate:
  • Bluetrust, reliability
  • Redenergy, excitement
  • Greennature, growth
  • Yellowhappiness, optimism
  • Black/Whiteluxury

AI Feedback

Directions for Step 4: What to include

  1. Company Name

    • Include your official company name at the top of your brand board.
    • Make it clear, legible, and consistent with your branding.

  2. Motto / Slogan

    • Include your company’s motto or slogan.

  3. Mission Statement / Company Reason

  4. Logos (3 versions)

    Design three versions of your logo:

    • Main Logo: Your full official logo with company name and tagline.
    • Simple Logo: A version with just the symbol or icon, no words.
    • Rectangle Logo: A horizontal or long rectangular version, suitable for banners or website headers.

    All logos should be consistent in style and color.

  5. Fonts

    • Define your primary font (for headings) and secondary font (for body text or smaller descriptions).
    • Show how the fonts look on the brand board.

  6. Color Palette

    • Include your full brand color palette (3 to 4 main colors plus any accent colors).
    • Label each color clearly (name or HEX code).
    • Make sure your colors match your logos and overall brand identity.

  7. Promotional Item

    • Include a visual of a promotional item with your logo. Examples: water bottle, t-shirt, mug, bag, or poster.
    • This can be hand-drawn, digitally created, or a photo mock-up.
    • Show how your logo and colors appear in a real-world product.

Format and presentation

  • All elements should fit on one page.
  • Layout should be neat, visually appealing, and easy to read.
  • Be creative but professional. This is your company’s “official brand board.”

Your Digital Presentation Must Include

0
AI Free

  1. Company Introduction

    Name, motto, mission statement, and what you do.

  2. The Problem and Your Solution

    What issue or need does your company solve?

  3. Target Audience

    Who will use or benefit from your product?

  4. The Product or Service

    Show or describe your mock-up or prototype.

  5. What Makes You Unique

    Why is your idea better or different?

  6. The Ask

    What support are you asking the Sharks for (funding, marketing, help)?

Tips

  • Keep your pitch under 5 minutes.
  • Everyone in your group should speak.
  • Practice until you sound confident and natural.

How to Connect it to Shark Tank

Entrepreneurs on the show

  • Introduce their business idea clearly.
  • Describe the problem and explain their solution.
  • Show their product prototype or a demonstration.
  • Share their numbers: how it works, costs, or why it’s valuable.
  • Make an ask for money or a partnership.
  • Answer questions confidently and stay professional.

In Your Presentation

  • You’ll show your physical brand board instead of business data.
  • You’ll explain your product idea, how it works, and why it matters.
  • You’ll make your ask (support, votes, or pretend “funding”).
  • You’ll use confident speaking and visuals to “wow” your Sharks!
